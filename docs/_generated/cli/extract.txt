Extract a feature table for a sample's calls, optionally labelled with truth

Usage: winnowr extract [OPTIONS] --vcf <VCF> --output <OUTPUT>

Options:
  -t, --threads <THREADS>              The number of worker threads to use [default: OMP_NUM_THREADS
                                       when set, else 4]
      --vcf <VCF>                      The indexed, single-sample VCF from HaplotypeCaller (gVCF
                                       mode, e.g. --dragen-mode, or a standard VCF with --alignments
                                       or --feature-source vcf)
  -o, --output <OUTPUT>                The feature table to write (Parquet)
      --gvcf <GVCF>                    The indexed gVCF the VCF was genotyped from. It supplies the
                                       raw annotations (RAW_MQandDP, QUALapprox, VarDP, AS_RAW_MQ)
                                       when the VCF lacks them, and is not read otherwise. Not used
                                       with --alignments or --feature-source vcf
      --feature-source <SOURCE>        Where the features of HaplotypeCaller's gVCF-mode annotations
                                       (mq, dp_norm, qd, as_alt_frac, sor, n_alt) come from: gvcf
                                       (the raw annotations of the VCF or of --gvcf), alignments
                                       (qd, sor and n_alt derived from the VCF's FORMAT/AD,
                                       FORMAT/PL and FORMAT/SB or INFO/SOR, and mq, dp_norm and
                                       as_alt_frac measured from the reads of --alignments; for a
                                       VCF without a gVCF), or vcf (as alignments, but with mq and
                                       dp_norm from the VCF's INFO/MQ and INFO/DP and as_alt_frac
                                       missing; for a VCF without a gVCF or reads). The table
                                       records it: models train only on tables of one source, and
                                       score calls with the features of theirs [default: alignments
                                       with --alignments, else gvcf] [possible values: gvcf, vcf,
                                       alignments]
      --alignments <ALIGNMENTS>        The indexed BAM or CRAM of the sample's reads, which the
                                       alignments feature source measures each call's mapping
                                       qualities and depth from (see --feature-source and
                                       --alignment-settings), on --contigs. Needs --reference
      --alignment-settings <SETTINGS>  Which reads of --alignments are counted at a call, as
                                       comma-separated settings over the defaults, each at most
                                       once: mapqN (only reads with MAPQ >= N: HaplotypeCaller's
                                       minimum, 20, or 1 in DRAGEN mode; not the high bin, as in
                                       `pileup --settings`), flankN (reads within N bases of the
                                       call's REF, and the aligned flank bases an indel read needs
                                       on each side of the repeat tract to be counted at an allele)
                                       and bqN (the minimum base quality of a SNP base counted at an
                                       allele). The table records them as part of its feature
                                       source: a model scores only calls measured as its training
                                       tables were [default: mapq20,bq10,flank5]
      --regions <NAME=PATH>...         Region BEDs to flag calls in, as NAME=PATH (e.g.
                                       segdups=segdups.bed.gz). The names must match those a model
                                       was trained with
      --contigs <CONTIGS>...           The contigs to extract [default: chr1 chr2 chr3 chr4 chr5
                                       chr6 chr7 chr8 chr9 chr10 chr11 chr12 chr13 chr14 chr15 chr16
                                       chr17 chr18 chr19 chr20 chr21 chr22]
      --sample <SAMPLE>                The sample name to record [default: the VCF's sample]
      --strlen <STRLEN>...             Repeat-tract read-length tables from `winnowr strlen` (e.g.
                                       one per shard), joined as repeat-length features of indel
                                       calls; needed to train or apply models that use them
      --pileup <PILEUP>...             Allele read count tables from `winnowr pileup` (e.g. one per
                                       shard), joined as pileup features of SNP and indel calls;
                                       needed to train or apply models that use them
      --dp-median <DP_MEDIAN>          The depth that the depth feature (dp_norm) divides each
                                       call's RAW_MQandDP depth (INFO/DP with --feature-source vcf,
                                       the reads counted with --alignments) by, in place of the
                                       median depth of the calls on --contigs [default: that
                                       median]; at least 1. Use it for calls of only part of the
                                       genome (e.g. a target footprint), whose median depth is
                                       skewed, with the median depth of the sample's genome-wide
                                       calls (which a whole-genome extract logs)
      --happy <HAPPY>                  The hap.py output VCF for this sample (run with --engine
                                       vcfeval, preferably with --pass-only; see --happy-pass-only),
                                       which labels PASS calls; needed for training and evaluation
                                       tables
      --happy-pass-only <MODE>         Whether hap.py was run with --pass-only: yes, no, or auto to
                                       infer it from the labels. It decides whether a --happy label
                                       at a non-PASS call's position is that call's own or a PASS
                                       call's that hap.py represented there [default: auto]
                                       [possible values: auto, yes, no]
      --truth-vcf <TRUTH_VCF>          The indexed truth VCF (e.g. a GIAB benchmark); with
                                       --truth-bed and --happy, labels non-PASS calls for training
                                       promote models
      --truth-bed <TRUTH_BED>          The truth confident-regions BED
      --reference <REFERENCE>          The indexed, uncompressed reference FASTA: needed by
                                       --alignments (also used to decode CRAM), and used to
                                       left-align indels when matching non-PASS calls to the truth
                                       (without it, alleles are only trimmed)
  -h, --help                           Print help
